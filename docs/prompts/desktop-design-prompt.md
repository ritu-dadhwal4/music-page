# Desktop design prompt

> Google Flow prompt used to generate `design/desktop.png` (1440 × 1024).
> Reproduced verbatim as written.

---

Design a premium desktop web interface for a personalised music playlist called "Nostalgia."

Create a single-page, full-screen music player with a warm, cinematic, vintage aesthetic. The design should feel like a personal digital mixtape made for someone special—not like Spotify, a dashboard, or a generic streaming platform.

## CANVAS AND STYLE

- Desktop viewport: 1440 × 1024 px.
- Use a warm ivory, cream, beige, taupe, and burnt-orange color palette.
- Main background: soft ivory with very subtle peach, beige, and golden blurred gradients.
- Add a small amount of warm film grain and tiny glowing square particles around the record.
- Use dark espresso brown for primary text and muted taupe for secondary text.
- Accent color: burnt orange, approximately `#C96322`.
- Use an elegant editorial serif font for branding and important song titles.
- Use a clean modern sans-serif font for secondary information and controls.
- Keep the interface spacious, minimal, refined, and nostalgic.
- Use thin borders, soft shadows, rounded corners, and translucent off-white surfaces.
- Do not add a sidebar, search bar, login buttons, navigation links, advertisements, dark mode, or unrelated content.

## HEADER

Create a slim header approximately 72 px high across the top.

- Place the word "Nostalgia" on the left with approximately 56 px horizontal padding.
- Use an elegant black serif logotype, around 27 px.
- Add a thin, short, light-beige vertical divider immediately after the word.
- Keep the rest of the header completely empty.
- Use a slightly lighter cream background than the main page.

## MAIN HERO AREA

Below the header, create a large hero section that occupies all remaining space above the playlist queue.

- Centre an oversized vinyl record in the hero area.
- The record should be approximately 720 px in diameter.
- Use a muted warm-grey/taupe surface with several subtle circular grooves.
- Add a large amber-peach centre label and a small dark spindle hole.
- The vinyl should feel softly illustrated and premium, not highly realistic or glossy.
- Add a subtle shadow underneath it.
- Place tiny, softly glowing peach and gold square particles around the record.

## FLOATING MUSIC PLAYER

Overlay a horizontal music-player card across the lower-middle section of the vinyl record.

- Card size: approximately 630 × 205 px.
- Use a translucent warm-white surface with a thin white border.
- Border radius: approximately 18 px.
- Add a soft, diffused shadow.
- Keep all content precisely aligned and uncluttered.

**Top row of the player:**

- On the far left, show a square album thumbnail with rounded corners, approximately 58 × 58 px.
- Beside it, show the song title "1901 (Acoustic French Wave)" in a bold serif font.
- Truncate the title with an ellipsis if needed.
- Under it, show "Phoenix / Summer Sunset Re-edit" in smaller muted sans-serif text.
- On the right, add controls in this order:
  1. Shuffle
  2. Previous track inside a small pale circular button
  3. Large burnt-orange circular Play button
  4. Next track inside a small pale circular button
  5. Repeat
- Use simple outlined icons.
- The orange Play button should be the visual focus and have a subtle shadow.

**Progress section:**

- Place a full-width audio progress slider under the top row.
- The completed portion should be burnt orange.
- The remaining portion should be light beige-grey.
- Add a small dark circular slider handle with a thin light outline.
- Show "1:24" below the left side and "3:48" below the right side using small monospaced text.

**Bottom row:**

- On the left, add a tiny orange equalizer icon followed by "Tape Saturation Warmth."
- On the right, add a speaker icon and a short volume slider.
- Use dark grey for the filled volume level and pale beige for the remainder.

## BOTTOM PLAYLIST QUEUE

Create a fixed horizontal queue panel across the entire bottom of the screen.

- Height: approximately 120 px.
- Background: warm translucent white.
- Add a faint top border.
- Add approximately 36 px horizontal padding.
- The queue should look like a horizontal carousel.

At the far left:

- Add a small orange queue icon.
- Show the uppercase label "UP NEXT."
- Beside it, show a small rounded pill containing an orange dot and the number "5."
- Add a thin vertical divider after this section.

Display five horizontal track cards:

1. "1901 (Acoustic)" — "Phoenix / Summer" — 3:48
2. "The Drifter's Path" — "Sarah Jenkins" — 4:18
3. "Golden Hour Daze" — "Aurora Skies" — 3:32
4. "Drifting Coast" — "Aurora Halo" — 4:05
5. "Cassette Reverie" — "Miles & The Echo" — 3:49

Each track card must include:

- A small square vintage album cover.
- Song title.
- Artist name underneath.
- Duration aligned to the right.
- A light cream background.
- Rounded corners and a very subtle border.

Highlight the currently selected first track using:

- A slightly peach-tinted background.
- A thin burnt-orange border.
- Orange song-title text.

Add a thin horizontal burnt-orange scroll-position indicator beneath the cards.

## INTERACTIONS

Create realistic prototype states for:

- Play and pause.
- Previous and next track.
- Shuffle and repeat.
- Audio progress.
- Volume adjustment.
- Clicking a song in the queue changes the active track.
- The active song card receives the orange highlight.
- Album artwork, song title, artist, timing, and player state update together.

The final screen must look like a polished, personal, nostalgic music experience with an oversized vinyl record as its central visual.
